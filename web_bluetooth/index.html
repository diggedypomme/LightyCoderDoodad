Quick test
  1. Power on the Arcade Coder.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Connect, choose the board, draw a few pixels, then send.

Only the chosen device is granted to this page. Bluetooth discovery must begin from your click.

PC recovery: Disconnect / forget drops this page's GATT connection and revokes its remembered permission. It cannot remove a Windows pairing; do that in Windows Bluetooth settings if the adapter remains stuck.
