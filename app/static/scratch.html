What this is

Blockly provides the open-source block workspace. Our compiler generates the small Arcade Engine JavaScript subset used by the stock firmware. Current blocks cover named sprites, movement, colour, visibility, edge events, directional tilt, shake, randomness and tick-based repetition. This is our interface, not a copy of the original app.
